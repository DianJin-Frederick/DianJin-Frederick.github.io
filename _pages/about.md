Hi, I am Dian Jin (金典), a third-year PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UW-Madison. I work with Prof. [Jeremy Coulson](https://jeremycoulson.github.io/) in data-driven control. 
My research focuses on data-driven control, subspace tracking, and optimization on manifolds, with the goal of developing methods that enable adaptive and robust control of complex dynamical systems.

Beyond research, I am passionate about photography and outdoor exploration — often combining my academic journey with travel and visual storytelling.
